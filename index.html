How This Was Built — AI Stack

This site itself is a demonstration of the AI stack, not just a description of it.

I used Claude as an AI pair-builder for this entire capstone — from structuring the site's content and design direction, to writing and debugging the HTML/CSS/JavaScript, to designing the logic behind the chatbot agent below.

Along the way I practiced: breaking a vague goal ("build a personal brand site + ship an agent") into clear, buildable steps; iterating on prompts to refine design and content; and understanding how a simple AI agent is structured — a knowledge base, a matching function, and a conversational interface — well enough to explain and extend it myself.

Tools & concepts applied: prompt iteration, AI-assisted front-end development, agent design (knowledge base + intent matching), and everyday AI tools — Claude AI, Arena AI, Qwen, and Perplexity.
